# Rabi field audit (descriptive)

Do the existing 240 sampled fields contain rabi farming? Script A selected fields with a crop cycle in the CALENDAR year (not kharif crops specifically), so they may include rabi fields. Per field and season (Oct - Apr), the month of highest NDVI: **rabi peak** = highest in Jan-Mar and > 0.35 (a crop green after the kharif harvest; it may have been sown in Oct-Nov); **late kharif** = highest in Oct-Nov; **December peak**; **never green** (never above 0.35). Historically rabi-active = rabi peak in >= 2 of the reference seasons 2019-20 .. 2022-23. No comparison with drought declarations is made here.
