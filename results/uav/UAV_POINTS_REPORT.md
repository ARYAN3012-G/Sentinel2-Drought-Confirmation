# Satellite view of the UAV survey fields

Sentinel-2 in a 25 m circle at each UAV survey point (same cleaning and SVI reference as the sampled fields: 2019-2023, own year excluded). UAV verdict = majority of the UAV team's labelled images (dataset table; not produced here). Satellite verdict = stressed if SVI < -1 on the last clear image before the flight or at any point in the 30 days before it.

Caution: the flights were in Nov 2025 - Jan 2026, partly after the kharif harvest. A harvested or drying maize field has low NDVI whatever the season, and SVI is unlabelled where the field has no normal crop signal at that time of year. Read disagreements with the crop stage in mind.
